    How Low Libido Treatment for Women Works in North Little Rock, AR

    What Causes Low Libido in North Little Rock

    Low libido in women rarely has a single cause. Common contributors include SSRIs and SNRIs, hormonal birth control, perimenopause, thyroid problems, chronic stress, poor sleep, pain during sex and relationship strain. So does how you feel in your body. Persistent, distressing low desire without another explanation is called hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. That label opens up FDA-approved options.

    Who Low Libido Treatment Is For

    This is for adult women bothered by a drop in desire or arousal. It often helps women whose interest changed after starting a medication, having a baby, entering perimenopause or going through a stressful stretch at work or home. You can start without a label. If lower desire does not bother you, there is nothing to fix, because distress is part of the definition. Your provider helps sort it out.

    Treatment Options and Schedules in North Little Rock

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is a 100 mg tablet taken every night at bedtime. PT-141, brand name Vyleesi, is a 1.75 mg self-injection taken at least 45 minutes before sex, with limits of once a day and eight times a month. Oxytocin is compounded. It is used off-label as a spray or troche before intimacy and is not FDA-approved. Some women need no medication at all.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly review. Early steps often include adjusting a medication, treating dryness or improving sleep, sometimes alongside a new prescription. PT-141 can work with the first dose. Flibanserin builds over four to eight weeks, and providers usually stop it at eight weeks if nothing has improved. Check-ins track the change.

    Side Effects in North Little Rock

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, fatigue and low blood pressure. Alcohol and certain interacting drugs make them stronger, which is why it is taken at bedtime with drinking limits. Nausea is common with PT-141. It can also cause flushing, headache, injection site reactions, a brief rise in blood pressure and darker skin patches with repeated use. Oxytocin can cause headache.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Liver disease rules out flibanserin. Drugs like fluconazole, clarithromycin and certain HIV medications rule it out, and drinking has to be kept away from the bedtime dose. PT-141 has different limits. Women with high blood pressure that is not controlled, or with heart disease, should not use PT-141. Neither is used during pregnancy.

    Comparing Low Libido Options in North Little Rock

    Flibanserin is daily and steady. It fits women who drink little and like a routine, with clinical trials showing a modest average gain of about one more satisfying encounter a month versus placebo. PT-141 is as-needed. Some women prefer that, though nausea can be a drawback. Oxytocin is gentler, with limited research.
